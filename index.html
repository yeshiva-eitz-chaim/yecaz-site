Fostering a Warm Environment Where Jewish Kids Thrive

At Yeshiva Eitz Chaim (YEC), our mission is to provide a warm, loving atmosphere where Jewish children feel like part of a family. Through exceptional Jewish and General Studies education in single-gender classrooms, we foster academic excellence while instilling good Middos to develop strong moral character. Rooted in a sense of community, we are dedicated to nurturing each child’s growth, preparing them to contribute meaningfully to their families, their communities, and the world.

Cultivating Each Child’s Unique Spark.

At Yeshiva Eitz Chaim, we recognize that every child carries a unique spark of potential. Our small class sizes allow for personalized attention, enabling us to tailor each student’s journey in Torah learning, Middos development, and General Studies. By nurturing their individuality and instilling a deep connection to Jewish values, we empower our students to thrive academically and spiritually. This individualized approach, rooted in our commitment to Torah and character, is what makes YEC a place where every child shines.

Discover if YEC is the Right Place for Your Child.

At Yeshiva Eitz Chaim, we cater to Jewish boys and girls from Kindergarten through 12th grade, providing an environment where their unique needs and values are embraced. We are under the guidance of our Rabbi, Harav Avrum Rabinowitz Shlita, and our Principal, Rabbi Amrum Hertzog.

Students are nurtured in their academic and spiritual growth, receiving a well-rounded education grounded in Jewish principles and Torah values. Our commitment to maintaining a supportive and focused atmosphere ensures that each child thrives both in their learning and character development.

Jewish Studies is at the heart of our curriculum. We focus on nurturing a deep love for Torah, Halacha, and Chassidus, ensuring that each student builds a strong spiritual foundation. Through engaging classes and hands-on learning, our students develop a meaningful connection to their heritage and values.

Our General Studies curriculum prepares students for academic success with a focus on core subjects like Math, English, Science, and History. We encourage critical thinking and problem-solving, helping students excel both in and outside the classroom.

Our extracurricular activities not only promote teamwork, creativity, and leadership but also emphasize the importance of Middos (moral character). Through various programs, students learn the value of respect, responsibility, and kindness, reinforcing the lessons they learn in the classroom.
