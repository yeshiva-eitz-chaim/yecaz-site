Arizona Tax Credit Program (STO)

Make a difference with your Arizona state tax dollars by supporting YEC through the STO program.

By taking advantage of the dollar-for-dollar Private School Tuition Tax Credit, you can direct your state taxes to fund scholarships for our students. It costs you nothing extra and provides essential support to our programs and initiatives.
